Croatian tax lists, 1495–1596

Taxable selišta (hearths) from the Kampuš–Adamček censuses of the Zagreb, Križevci, Varaždin and Virovitica counties.

—

Circle area ∝ taxable selišta that year. Marker colour shows how confident the location is; places with no coordinates are scattered around their county town.

Total taxable selišta per county per census. The frontier counties' base collapses across the century as Ottoman raids depopulate the region — Križevci falls from ~18,000 (1507) to under 100.

Croatian tax lists

A structured, queryable edition of the early-modern tax censuses of Zagreb, Križevci, Varaždin and Virovitica counties, 1495-1596.

This project turns the Excel transcription of Ivan Kampuš & Josip Adamček's Popisi i obračuni poreza u Hrvatskoj u XV. i XVI. stoljeću into a clean, queryable dataset — every taxpayer holding, place and levy from 23 census campaigns.

All of the data is openly available, as both a ready-to-query SQLite database and plain CSV files, at github.com/tombali/populus-early-modern-croatia.
